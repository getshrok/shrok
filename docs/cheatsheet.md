# Cheatsheet

Besides asking it to do something directly, anything you want to do with Shrok can be covered by one or more of these:

- Identity files are for info Shrok should always know or keep in mind
- Skills are for teaching Shrok new capabilities
- Tasks are for something you want Shrok to do in the future (on-demand, recurring, or once)
- Reminders are for time-based nudges — one-off or recurring; can be made acknowledgment-required so they keep nagging until you handle them
- Sensors are for making Shrok always aware of live info without spending time or tokens looking it up

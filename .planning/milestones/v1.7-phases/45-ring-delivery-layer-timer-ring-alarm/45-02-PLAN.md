<objective>
Build the headless ring delivery core: the per-channel persisted ring-state store and the `RingRunner` class. The runner derives the device's `media_player` + LED `light` from the satellite via the HA template API (cached), sets volume, plays the bundled beep, lights the LED, then runs a `setInterval` poll that replays the beep on idle — with ZERO LLM/queue activation in the loop. `stop()` tears everything down; a 24h cap is the safety backstop; the store enables precise restart cleanup.

Purpose: This is the load-bearing "make noise until told to stop" mechanism shared by timers and alarms. The hard constraint (no model in the loop) lives here.
Output: `src/ring/store.ts`, `src/ring/runner.ts`, and their test files (the Wave-0 scaffolds named in 45-VALIDATION.md).
</objective>

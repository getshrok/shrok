<objective>
Rework the runner into the dual-sink producer and wire the head + queue through to it:
- `runSensor` parses stdout as exactly one JSON object `{ ambient?, event? }`, routes `ambient`→`ambient/<headId>/<slug>.md` (write-file-atomic), routes a well-formed `event`→`enqueue({type:'sensor_event', ...}, PRIORITY.SENSOR_EVENT, headId)`, and reuses the existing failure-marker path for process failure AND malformed/non-object stdout.
- The runner gains a `headId` parameter and a narrow enqueue-sink dependency.
- The scheduler `kind:'script'` branch passes `schedule.headId`.
- The `sensorRunner` closure in `src/index.ts` closes over the existing `queue` and threads `headId`.

</objective>

<objective>
Eliminate the long-lived in-memory `history` array in `src/sub-agents/local.ts`. Restructure `loopIteration` to rebuild history from the DB (`AgentStore.getHistoryWithinBudget`) at the point it is about to run a tool-loop pass, drop the buffer when the loop parks, unify the two `update()` resume paths onto a DB-sourced read, and retire the `work_start` index in favor of the `!m.injected` filter. This converges sub-agents onto the head's model (DB is canonical; in-memory is a transient per-`runToolLoop` buffer).

Purpose: Single source of truth for sub-agent history; idle agents hold zero conversation in memory; the two resume paths stop diverging.
Output: A refactored `loopIteration`/`runLoopFrom`/`runLoop`/`resumeSuspended`/`update()` in `local.ts`. `runToolLoop` (`src/llm/tool-loop.ts`) is UNCHANGED. Restart reaping (`src/index.ts:349–358`) is UNCHANGED.
</objective>
